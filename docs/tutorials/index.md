# Tutorials: a Week With the App

Seven short days, for someone who has never traded anything and never
written code. Each day takes about fifteen minutes in the app and ends
with one thing you can now do. Nothing here uses real money: every
balance is play money, and the app is a tool for building and testing
ideas, not advice.

The app is not open to the public yet. It runs for its developers while
it is built (`docs/platform.md`). These pages describe it as it is, and
they are kept in step with it.

| Day | You will | Where in the app |
| ---: | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | [Watch one market](day1_watch.md) and read its price as a chance | Markets |
| 2 | [See how a forecast is scored](day2_scores.md), and why the market is the bar | Learn |
| 3 | [Run a backtest](day3_backtest.md) on a sample strategy | Backtests |
| 4 | [Describe a strategy in a sentence](day4_describe.md) and read it back | Strategies → Describe |
| 5 | [Preview it and paper trade it](day5_paper.md) | a strategy's page |
| 6 | [Read your own record](day6_record.md): health, risk and readiness | Strategies → Risk |
| 7 | [Compare and share](day7_share.md) | Leaderboards, sharing |

Two reading levels run through everything: **Simple** says things in
sentences, and **Detailed** adds the numbers, formulas and tables. You can
switch at any time in Settings. Start in Simple.
