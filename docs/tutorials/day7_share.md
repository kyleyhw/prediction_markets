# Day 7: Compare and Share

**You will:** see how others' strategies do, and show yours.

1. **Leaderboards** rank strategies by skill against the market on their
   settled paper positions, not by money made, so a lucky long shot does
   not top the table. Nothing is ranked before 50 settled positions.
   Entering is your choice, and you choose the name shown.
2. From your strategy's page you can **share** it: a public page with what
   you allow (the rules, the record, your name). Anyone can **fork** it,
   which makes their own copy, and your original is never changed.
3. **Comments** on strategies and markets let a team discuss them. In
   **Team** you can invite people to work in the same place, each with a
   role.
4. **Notifications** and, in Settings, **Delivery** send you what you ask
   for (a brief each morning, a notice when a strategy is paused) by
   email or chat, with quiet hours.

That is the week. From here, the [docs](../index.md) go deeper, and the
[Research Lab](../lab/index.md) shows what the platform has measured,
each number with the command that reproduces it.
