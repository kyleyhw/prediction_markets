# Day 5: Preview It, Then Paper Trade It

**You will:** see what your strategy would do today, then let it trade
with play money.

1. Your strategy's page shows a **preview**: how many markets it would
   look at right now, how many settled markets a month it covers, what it
   would cost, and how many bets an edge needs before anyone could tell it
   from luck.
2. **Backtest** it, as on Day 3. Its run card lists its results with the
   caveats that apply, such as a small number of markets.
3. **Paper trade** it. The platform then runs it every hour on the real
   markets with play money: it forecasts, bets when it sees an edge, pays
   each market's real fee, and settles when the market does. It keeps
   going while your computer is off.
4. Every bet and settlement goes into a **ledger** that cannot be changed
   without it showing. In Detailed you can download it and check it
   yourself.

Paper trading is the honest test: nobody, including the strategy, knows
the answers yet.
