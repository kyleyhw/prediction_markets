# Day 6: Read Your Own Record

**You will:** know whether a strategy is still doing what it did, and how
much it could lose.

1. On your strategy's page, **health** is a pill and a sentence. It
   watches whether the strategy keeps beating the market as its bets
   settle. If it stops, the strategy is **paused**: it opens no new
   positions, its open ones still settle, and you are told. You decide
   whether to resume it.
2. **Risk** (from Strategies) looks at all your open positions together,
   because bets on the same match or the same day's weather win or lose
   together. In Simple it says the most you could lose this week and what
   you would stay under in 19 weeks out of 20.
3. **Readiness** lists the numbered criteria a strategy must meet before
   it could ever go further, each met or not. None of the platform's own
   strategies meets them, and the page says why.
4. If you already trade on the venue, **Your record** reads your public
   trading history as a report card: whether the market later agreed with
   you, and your costliest habit.
