# Day 1: Watch One Market

**You will:** read a market's price as the chance of something happening,
and see what it costs to buy.

1. The first time you sign in, the **guided start** asks what interests
   you (weather, football, esports and so on) and which sample strategy
   Home should follow. Pick anything; every choice can be changed later.
2. Open **Markets**. Each card is a question with a yes-or-no answer that
   will be settled later, for example whether a team wins on Sunday.
3. The bar and the percentage are the **price** of a "yes" share. A share
   pays $1 if the answer turns out yes and nothing if not, so a price of
   62¢ means the people trading think the chance is about 62%. That is
   the market's forecast.
4. Every card says the **fee to buy**, for example "about 1.2¢ a share".
   The venue sets it for each market, and it is largest near 50¢.
5. Open a card. The market's page shows how the chance has moved and what
   the sample strategies think it will be.

**Try:** pick one market that closes within a few days and write down its
chance. Come back after it settles and see what happened. One market says
little: a 70% chance still fails three times in ten.
